## Monomials (The Ingredients)
In mathematics, a monomial is a single "block" of math where 
numbers and variables are **strictly multiplied** together—**no addition or subtraction**.


## Polynomials (The Recipe)
A polynomial is simply a collection of **monomials added or subtracted together**.
    In the context of encryption:
    The polynomials ($f_1, f_2, \dots, f_n$) are the actual Public Key. 
    They act as the "recipes" for scrambling.

## Lexicographical Order (The Formatting)

Lexicographical order is just the mathematical term for "dictionary order."
Lexicographical order is the universally agreed-upon **sorting system**. 
It ensures that when the polynomial "recipe" is transmitted over the internet,
every computer reads the terms in the exact same sequence, preventing the scrambling 
instructions from getting jumbled.
